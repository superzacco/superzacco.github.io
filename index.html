Ano's Blade: Akuara Nil Stories -- a story
The first in an anthology of stories set within my sci-fi universe, Akuara Nil.

Ano is a sentient android working as a fixer for the stupidly rich business magnate, Tuxley Vance. His mission was to facilitate a deal between his boss and the subjugating crime lord of this sector, Vasquez.

Deals rarely go right on the lawless planet of Tantua Prime, and Ano finds himself in a conflict far bigger than he expected. Vasquez then sets his sights on Tuxley, up at his headquarters on the planet Hyphnion.

With Tuxley's life in danger, Ano has to race across the galaxy to reach his headquarters, fighting through over a hundred floors to get to his boss' office before Vasquez can.
